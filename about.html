Who We Are

A Brand Born from India's Ancient Wellness Traditions

PranVeda — meaning "elixir of the Vedas" — was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that India's ancient Ayurvedic knowledge holds the key to balanced, sustainable wellness. In an age of synthetic shortcuts and quick fixes, we chose a different path.

Our founders, deeply inspired by the Ayurvedic traditions passed down through generations in their own families, set out to create products that honour the integrity of these ancient formulations while meeting the quality expectations of today's discerning wellness consumer.

Every PranVeda product is a promise — a promise of purity, of honest ingredients, of genuine care for your wellbeing.

Our Journey

How PranVeda Grew

2019
The Beginning

PranVeda was founded by a small passionate team in New Delhi, inspired by family Ayurvedic recipes passed down through generations. We launched our first three products — Brahmi Hair Oil, Amla Shampoo Powder, and Triphala Churna.

2020
Growing Community

Word spread through WhatsApp and social media as customers shared their experiences. We crossed 1,000 happy customers and expanded our product range to include skincare and immunity products.

2021
Expanding Across India

PranVeda began delivering PAN India. We partnered with certified Ayurvedic herb farmers across Rajasthan, Kerala, and Uttarakhand to ensure the freshest, most potent raw materials.

2022
Premium Formulations

We launched our premium Kumkumadi skincare range and expanded into wellness tonics and herbal powders. Our customer base grew to over 5,000 and we maintained a 4.8+ star rating across all products.

Today
10,000+ Happy Customers

With over 50 products and 10,000+ satisfied customers across India, PranVeda continues to grow — staying true to our roots, our values, and our unwavering commitment to authentic Ayurvedic wellness.
