How to Use

1
Warm the oil gently

Pour a small amount into a bowl and warm it slightly (not hot). Warm oil penetrates the scalp more effectively.

2
Apply to scalp

Part your hair into sections and apply the oil directly to the scalp using your fingertips.

3
Massage for 10–15 minutes

Use gentle circular motions with your fingertips to massage the scalp for improved circulation and absorption.

4
Leave for minimum 2 hours

For best results, leave the oil on overnight. Wrap hair with a warm towel to enhance absorption.

5
Wash with mild shampoo

Rinse with lukewarm water and a gentle, sulphate-free shampoo. Use 2–3 times per week for best results.

Storage Instructions

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Keep the cap tightly closed. Do not refrigerate. Best used within 24 months of manufacture date (printed on bottle).
