Return & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2024

At Vedamrit, we strive to provide you with the highest quality premium Ayurvedic products. Since our products are natural wellness and personal care formulations, we follow a strict return policy to maintain hygiene and safety standards.

Returns and Exchanges

We accept returns or exchanges within 7 days of delivery only under the following circumstances:

  • The product received was damaged during transit.
  • The product received is incorrect or does not match your order confirmation.
  • The product has a manufacturing defect or quality issue.

To request a return or exchange, please send a message to us on WhatsApp at +91 99999 99999 or email us at hello@vedamrit.in with your order details and an unboxing video or photographs showing the issue.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for returns or refunds:

  • Products that have been opened, used, or tampered with.
  • Products returned without their original premium packaging, tags, or labels.
  • Products purchased during special promotional sales or clearance events.

Refund Process

Once your return request is approved and the product is received back at our warehouse, we will verify the condition of the product. Upon successful verification, we will process your refund:

  • For prepaid orders, the refund will be credited back via UPI or Bank Transfer to your account within 5-7 business days.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, we will request your bank details or UPI ID on WhatsApp to transfer the refund amount.

Cancellations

You can cancel your order at any time before it is dispatched. Once the order has been handed over to our logistics partner, it cannot be cancelled. Please contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible if you need to cancel your order.

Contact

For any return-related assistance, email us at hello@vedamrit.in or WhatsApp us at +91 99999 99999.
